# Capability Map

## Model

A capability is not a claim by itself.

Use:

**CAPABILITY → EVIDENCE → PROJECT → ENTERPRISE → COMMERCIAL / PROFESSIONAL OUTCOME**

## Capability domains

### Business & Enterprise
- business development
- enterprise orchestration
- operations coordination
- procurement / sourcing support
- market-entry support

### Agriculture & Animal Science
- animal science
- livestock systems
- animal production
- agriculture
- farm management support
- research and knowledge synthesis

### Digital
- digital strategy
- web/PWA systems
- automation
- digital asset management
- AI-assisted workflows
- information architecture

### Research & Innovation
- research coordination
- evidence synthesis
- innovation discovery
- experimental project structuring
- knowledge management

### Commercialisation
- opportunity identification
- customer/problem mapping
- service/product structuring
- partner coordination
- recurring-revenue design
- digital asset monetisation

## Evidence requirement

Each capability must eventually link to one or more:

- qualification
- employment/engagement
- completed project
- repository
- published work
- documented outcome
- client evidence where permission exists

Avoid publishing a large capability list with no evidence.
