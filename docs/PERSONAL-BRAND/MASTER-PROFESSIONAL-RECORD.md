# Master Professional Record

This is the canonical content model for the public professional profile and future CV variants.

- Professional location/context: Botswana
- Public professional positioning: to be evidence-checked and refined

## 2. Professional profile

- sourcing / procurement / market-entry support

- research / innovation
- project and operations coordination

## 3. Education

For every qualification record:

| Institution | Programme | Dates | Outcome | Evidence | Public wording |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Include completed qualifications separately from incomplete studies.

## 4. Continuing education

Record:

- provider
- course / learning path
- completion status
- date
- certificate evidence, if available
- public wording

Do not call a course a certification unless certification evidence exists.

## 5. Professional experience

| Organisation / venture | Role | Period | Responsibilities | Outcomes | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Distinguish employment, consulting, founding, volunteering, project participation and client support.

## 6. Capabilities

Capabilities should be connected to evidence:

**Capability → Evidence → Project → Enterprise → Opportunity**

## 7. Projects

Each project record should include:

- problem
- role
- work performed
- technology/process
- outcome
- evidence
- current status
- canonical enterprise/repository

## 8. Research and knowledge

Capture:

- research
- publications
- technical notes
- field observations
- datasets
- experiments
- intellectual-property candidates

## 9. Enterprises

Only verified current enterprises should appear in the active enterprise layer.

Historical ideas belong in the timeline/archive.

## 10. Evidence model

Every substantive claim should have one of:

- VERIFIED
- DOCUMENTED
- SELF-REPORTED
- HISTORICAL
- UNVERIFIED
- REQUIRES CONFIRMATION

## 11. Update protocol

When new evidence is added:

1. Update the master record.
2. Update the evidence register.
3. Update the relevant asset/ownership record.
4. Update the canonical enterprise mapping.
5. Only then publish or redesign the corresponding UI.
